01

A six-machine home agentic network

I designed and operate a guardrails-first network of AI agents across six machines, used daily by non-technical family members. Allowlist/deny permission architecture, human-approval gates for anything destructive or outward-facing, an offline-queued message hub, wake-on-mention agents, and self-built MCP servers -- including a from-scratch OAuth 2.1 remote-MCP stack (RFC 8414/9728, PKCE).

  • machines in one governed fleet6
  • real use by non-technical usersdaily
  • destructive, outward-facing, and spend actionshuman-gated
